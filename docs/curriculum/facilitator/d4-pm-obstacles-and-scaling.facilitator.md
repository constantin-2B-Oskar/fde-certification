## Objective

Participants leave able to (1) recognize the technical and people failures of an FDE project early and act within 48 hours, and (2) plan a multi-site scale-up with humility, clear owners, aligned incentives, respect for local culture, and IT and cybersecurity involved from day one.

## Cards: what usually works, and the trap

**01. The weekend crew.** Works: go on Saturday, in person, with Marc if he agrees; ask what the weekend crew would need; make the crew leader the owner of the weekend version. Hour 48: the plant manager decides the standard; the crew leader agrees to a two-week trial. Trap: escalating to Hervé first, or saying "the data shows your crew loses 740 tonnes".

**02. The canteen.** Works: tell Hervé yourself the same day, without naming who said it; go and see the maintenance manager and ask what went wrong in 2022; ship one fix maintenance asked for, this week. Trap: arguing in the canteen, or defending the project by email. On "cut jobs": the FDE does not decide this and must not promise; the gains are framed as throughput, and HR speaks to jobs.

**03. The silent sponsor.** Works: one short written message with one ask and one date ("a call to group security by Thursday, or we slip by six weeks"); go through his assistant or catch him on the floor; if nothing by hour 48, escalate to whoever sold the project. Trap: working around him with Nadia or group IT, or waiting. This is the card where "sponsor not sponsor enough is a sales problem" lands.

**04. No time.** Works: ask Hervé, not Corinne, for two named hours; take twenty minutes at her desk on the ten rules that matter for Friday; give her something back first. Trap: sending her the forty rules by email "to validate when you can".

**05. Six weeks.** Works: submit the review today, with Nadia; plan six weeks of work on files Nadia can drop in an EU folder on the client tenant; tell Hervé the date moves, with the new date. Trap: a personal cloud account, a shadow copy of the data, or "we will do the paperwork later".

**06. The wrong baseline.** Works: Mireille hears it from you before Friday, in person, with the corrected number and the check you added; Hervé hears it first, the same day. Trap: waiting for Friday, or presenting the corrected number without saying it changed.

**07. The wrong theory.** Works: refuse to write slides for a conclusion the data does not support, without saying "you are wrong"; pre-wire him alone; give him the better story ("we found what eats our capacity"); "the APS decision belongs to the group; our results will make it a better decision". Trap: complying, or correcting him in front of the COO.

**08. The re-keying.** Works: listen first; "I do not decide your role; here is what I know, and here is who decides"; ask what errors she catches that SAP never shows; one next step she chooses. Trap: "nothing will change", or any promise about her job. This is the card where "get people to face their responsibilities" from Part 2 is first felt: HR and Hervé owe her an answer, not the FDE.

## Scale plan: what a strong poster contains

- **Humble.** It does not copy Lyon. Haguenau's problem is orders, so planning is not its first product; Évreux is sold out, so capacity and changeovers may pay; Tarragona's money is in energy and it has paper data, so the first product there is data capture, not a solver.
- **Responsibilities.** A named business owner and IT owner per plant; HR named for any role change; the plant controller validates the gain.
- **Incentives.** Each plant manager's win stated in his own KPI; where a plant wins nothing, the poster says so.
- **Local culture.** Tarragona in Spanish, with a new plant manager who needs a quick visible win; Haguenau's daily management used as the ritual, not replaced. Anything that contradicts the target (no willingness to share data, plans not followed) is listed as a sales problem for Laurent.
- **IT from day one.** Data on Polymex's servers, in the EU; one group security review for the platform rather than one per site; site IT trained on the ontology; the ontology core reused, connectors rebuilt (AutoTrack, the small Évreux MES, a capture form at Tarragona).
- **Order.** A defended choice. Évreux first (data exists, money is visible, line 7 in September) or Tarragona first (biggest euro gap) are both defensible; Haguenau first rarely is.
