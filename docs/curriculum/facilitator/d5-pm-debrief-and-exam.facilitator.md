# Day 5 afternoon: debrief, principles and the exam. Facilitator guide

*AI 50%: drafted by Claude from the FDE School curriculum and OSS engagement records, validated by the trainer.* Trainer: Renan. 13:30 to 17:30. Deck: `D5 PM - Deck - Debrief and exam.dc.html` (18 slides; content in `D5 PM - Deck content.md`). Handout: `D5 PM - Exercises.md` (30/60/90 template and feedback form). The traps of each case: `d5-am-the-full-motion.facilitator.md`.

## Objectives

1. Each participant learns from the four companies they did not work on.
2. The room sees what pre-sales got wrong across the five cases, and draws the four lessons of a kickoff.
3. Each participant writes a 30/60/90 plan for their first deployment.
4. Everyone sits the FDE Certification exam in good conditions.

## Slide notes

**Slides 4 and 5.** Never confirm which companies the cases come from. If a participant from one of them speaks, thank them and move on without adding detail. Fill slide 5 with the room's own examples before showing it.

**Slide 6.** Ask each team which of the four sentences their kickoff got right.

**Slide 8.** The principles map one to one to the week. Ask which one they think they will break first on a real site.

**Slide 9.** Same sentence as Day 1, slide 42. Point it out.

**Slide 14.** Read it word for word. If a connection drops, the participant reopens the page in the same browser and continues; the server keeps the time. Failed attempts can be retaken after 24 hours; give the date and time to anyone who asks.

## Exam logistics checklist

- Wi-Fi tested at 14:30 with twenty connections; a backup hotspot ready.
- Power strips at every table.
- Password `fdecompany` on the whiteboard only during the exam; erased at 16:45.
- AI tools forbidden: laptops in exam mode, no other windows; you walk the room.
- Results: note who passed, without announcing scores in the room.

## Close (16:45)

1. Feedback form (handout), ten minutes, before certificates.
2. Certificates for those who passed; for the others, a retake date and a sentence of encouragement in private.
3. Last words: the five things they can now do (slide 17), and the 30/60/90 plan they keep.
