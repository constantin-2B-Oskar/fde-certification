What the stated process gets wrong The week is not frozen: half of Friday's plan moves by Wednesday, and the change form is not used. Changeovers depend on the sequence, lines do not all run every family, and none of it is in SAP. SAP is not the plan; it is yesterday's copy of a workbook.
The shape of the true process Two loops run by one person: a full replan once a day over all lines, and two to four local repairs a day over the next 48 hours. The target follows it: a long optimizer daily, a short optimizer at every event, with the rules out of Corinne's head and into an engine she owns.
