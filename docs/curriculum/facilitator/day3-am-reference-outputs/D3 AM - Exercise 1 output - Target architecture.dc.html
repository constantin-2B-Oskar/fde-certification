Which kind of model architecture, and why
One autonomous agentThe model chooses each step and calls the solvers when it wants. Every step it chooses must be evaluated; a wrong step stops a line. Not for the plan.Rejected
A swarm of agentsA reader, a planner, a critic in conversation. Errors compound across hand-offs and nobody can tell Julien which agent decided. Overhead for a problem whose steps are known.Rejected for the loops
A workflow with models insideThe orchestrator fixes the steps. Jev picks among known options, a small LLM fills schemas, solvers decide, a frontier LLM explains. Every output is typed and checked; every decision has an owner.The target
Agents, offline onlyWhere the steps cannot be known in advance and nothing reaches the floor: mining rules from the notebooks and interviews, the weekly review of deviations. They propose; Corinne validates.Kept, read-only
