# Day 2: main feedback, and what changes

Thank you for your feedback on Day 2. Here are the three points that came up most, and our answers.

## 1. "It's a lot, and it's intense"

**What we heard:** the day was dense, and the pace was high.

**Our answer:** yes. Deal with it, and buckle up.

Said with a smile, but we mean it. In five days you cover what an FDE learns over months in the field: the role, the business, the technology, the obstacles and the full motion. Day 3 is just as intense, on purpose. The pre-read for each day is there so you can see the material beforehand and arrive with less to take in on the spot.

## 2. "The slides don't have enough pictures"

**What we heard:** the slides were heavy on text and light on images.

**What changes:** we changed the slides, and they now have pictures.

## 3. "Can you put what you drew on the paperboard into the material?"

**What we heard:** some of the most useful figures were drawn live on the paperboard, and they are not in the slides.

**What changes:** all the figures from the paperboard will be included in the material at the end of the week.
