## Main terms

**Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)**: An engineer who embeds in the client's operation and stays accountable for a measured result in production. Not a consultant, a solutions engineer or a staffing resource.

**The last mile**: Where industrial AI fails. The model is rarely the problem; the data, the workflow and the people are. The FDE closes those three gaps.

**Validated gain**: A gain the client's finance team has signed, computed by the controller from a protocol agreed in week one. It is the first thing an FDE is measured on.

**Ontology**: A formal model of what exists in the plant, how things relate, which rules apply and which actions are allowed. The model is rented; the ontology is the client's own asset.

**Bottleneck (constraint)**: The stage that limits the output of the whole plant. At Polymex you find it from the data: the stage that is always busy and never waits.

**OEE**: Overall equipment effectiveness: availability × performance × quality. It shows how much of planned time produced good output. Sum minutes and tonnes first, then divide; never average ratios row by row.

**Changeover**: Switching a line from one product to another, during which it makes no good output. At Polymex, changeovers rose from 49 to 71 a week between January and March.

**OTIF**: On time in full: the share of orders delivered on the promised date and in full. Polymex's OTIF fell from 91% to 84%, against a 95% target.

**Hypothesis tree**: A tree that starts from a bet about the cause, with a prior on each branch and a test that could kill it. Branches must be MECE: no overlaps, no gaps.

**TIP one-pager**: The contract of the first two weeks: problem statement, baseline and gap, hypothesis tree, tests, and the decision point.

**Answer first (the pyramid)**: Start with one governing thought, the answer and its so-what, then three supporting arguments, then the evidence.

**Action title**: A slide title that states the conclusion, such as "M07 runs 10 points slower than the fleet", not a topic like "Machine performance".

**Knowledge bundle**: One folder per engagement, in the Open Knowledge Format, where every email, file, meeting and decision is filed the same day. The client owns it.

**Sponsor**: The person with authority over the people who must change. At Polymex, Hervé, the site director. Each person who must change also needs their direct boss as a sponsor.

**Curve of change**: The stages people go through when their work changes, from shock and denial to integration. Morale dips before it rises, and each person moves at their own speed.

## The ideas to hold on to

- The model is a commodity; deployment is the scarce skill.
- An FDE is accountable for a measured result in production, not for a deliverable.
- Every number you show must survive someone else's check.
- An FDE is judged on decisions other people take, so put the answer first.
- Nobody should hear news about their own job second.

## Where this day fits in the week

Nothing comes before this day. It sets out the role, the toolchain, the plant and the habits you use all week: checked numbers, answer-first messages and one source of truth. Day 2 prices the leaks you find today in euros and turns them into a project. Days 3 and 4 build on your Plant Pulse app and on the people you meet today. Friday applies it all to two real cases and the FDE Certification exam.

## Questions to think about before the session

1. In an operation you know, which small daily decision costs the most when it goes wrong, and who takes it?
2. Think of a tool or project that was live but not used. Was the cause the data, the workflow or the people?
3. When you last brought news to a manager, did you start with the answer or with the background?
