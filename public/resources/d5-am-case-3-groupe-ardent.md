## Strategic positioning

- **Where it wins.** A portfolio of well-known brands, constant product innovation, and plants in Europe that support a "made in France" promise on cookware.
- **The repairability promise.** Spare parts available for 15 years and a network of repair centers: a strong argument with consumers and regulators.
- **The pressures.** Low-cost Asian competitors and retailers' own brands push prices down. Aluminium and energy costs are high, and the coating ovens are among the group's largest energy users. Professional coffee grows fast and earns the best margins; cookware earns the least.
- **What the exco wants.** Keep the French cookware plants competitive without moving production abroad.

## What was sold in pre-sales

The contract was signed by the Group COO. It covers **12 weeks at the cookware plant in Savoie**, with a team of four FDEs (you).

**Scope in the signed proposal:** "Reduce scrap on the six non-stick coating lines. AI-based visual inspection detects coating defects in real time; a model links defects to process settings so operators can correct them before the next pan."

**What is not in the proposal:** the plant has no cameras on the lines today. A camera project (about €1.2M of investment) was presented to the capex committee last spring and postponed.
