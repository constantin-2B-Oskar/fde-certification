# Day 4 afternoon: exercises

FDE School, by Devoteam and OSS Ventures. Participant handout. Two exercises: eight situation cards (14:05 to 15:25), then a scale plan from Lyon to three plants (16:10 to 17:10). Same teams of four; roles rotate as usual.

## Exercise 1. Situation cards: your next 48 hours

**How it works.** Each team draws two cards. Twenty minutes per card. For each card, fill one sheet with the template below. Readout: two minutes per card, then the room challenges with the four readout questions.

**Template, one sheet per card**

| | |
|---|---|
| Who you see first, and why that person | |
| Your first sentence, word for word | |
| Hour 0 to 4 | |
| Hour 4 to 24: what the sponsor hears from you | |
| Hour 24 to 48: what is written, and to whom | |
| What you will not do or say | |
| Agreed at hour 48: a name, an act, a date | |

**Readout questions**
1. Did the first person you saw have the problem, or the power to solve it?
2. Was your first sentence about their goal, or about yours?
3. Did you tell the sponsor before he heard it from someone else?
4. Is the hour-48 agreement specific?

## Exercise 2. The scale plan: Lyon to three plants

**Situation.** Lyon is live: long and short solvers, the rules validated by Corinne, permissions per role, the scorecard, Nadia's five conditions met. Laurent, the COO, asks for a plan to take it to Haguenau, Évreux and Tarragona. He will read it before the board's footprint review.

**What you know** (Day 2 knowledge bundle: plant files, capacity 2025, interviews 06, 07, 24, 25, 26)

| | Haguenau | Évreux | Tarragona |
|---|---|---|---|
| Capacity used, 2025 | 64%, line H5 at about 30% | 97%, line 7 from September 2026 | 78% |
| Data | AutoTrack, a real MES | Small MES since 2021 | Paper |
| Lean | The model plant, 3.3 out of 4 | Middle | Almost nothing |
| Main problem | No orders; a carmaker program ends in 2026 | Sold out, €1.7M turned down | Energy: 830 kWh per tonne against 640 at Lyon; price war |
| Plant manager | Thierry Muller | Claire Dubois | Jordi Puig, new |

Group IT: SAP ECC on every site, group security review of about six weeks for any new cloud service, data in the EU.

**Your poster.** One per team, with six blocks:

1. **Per plant, what it wins.** Is Lyon's planning system the right first product there? If not, what is?
2. **Owners.** For each plant: the business owner, the IT owner, who decides roles (HR), who validates the gain.
3. **Reuse and rebuild.** What you take from Lyon as is, and what you rebuild locally.
4. **IT and security.** Where the data lives, how site IT is trained on the ontology, when the security review starts, how secrets and access are handled.
5. **Order and dates.** Which plant first, and why.
6. **What you will not do, and the sales problems.** What you refuse to promise, and what you must take back to Laurent because it is not solvable on the floor.

**Judged against the five principles.** Humble, responsibilities, incentives, local culture, IT from day one. The room asks each team: which principle is weakest on your poster?
