# Day 4: main feedback, and what changes

Thank you for your feedback on Day 4. Here are the four points that came up most, and what we changed.

## 1. "The theory blocks were too long"

**What we heard:** too much time listening, not enough time doing.

**What changes:** on Day 5 the morning starts with a fifteen-minute brief, then ninety minutes of team work and an hour of presentations. For the next sessions, no theory block runs longer than twenty minutes before an exercise.

## 2. "Put more exercises in the morning"

**What we heard:** the morning is when you have the most energy, and it should be spent practicing.

**What changes:** the Day 5 morning is almost entirely an exercise: five teams, five companies, one kickoff each in front of an executive committee. For the next sessions, each morning has an exercise before 10:00.

## 3. "Show us a real ontology"

**What we heard:** the ontology stayed abstract; you want to see one used in production.

**What changes:** the Resources page now lists, under "Further reading", "Ontology and data", two videos of a real ontology in production (an overview, and an oil and gas infrastructure case), the documentation that defines its parts, and the research showing what an ontology changes for a language model's answers.

## 4. "Do a simple example of an ontology"

**What we heard:** before Lyon, a smaller example would help you see the idea.

**What changes:** a new one-page guide, "A simple ontology, step by step", builds the ontology of a two-oven bakery in five steps (things, facts, links, rules, events and actions), then maps the same five steps onto Lyon planning, and ends with a twenty-minute exercise on a process you know. It is on Day 5 and at the top of "Further reading".
