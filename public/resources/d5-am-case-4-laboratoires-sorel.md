## Strategic positioning

- **Where it wins.** Dermatology expertise and the trust of pharmacists. Products made in its own French plants, under pharmaceutical standards, even for cosmetics.
- **What is changing.** International growth, in Asia and the Americas, needs reliable supply and fast launches. Big beauty groups are moving into the pharmacy channel.
- **The pressures.** Good manufacturing practice (GMP) rules make every change slow and documented. Working capital is high: finished goods wait in quarantine until quality releases them.
- **What the foundation expects.** Long-term growth, local employment in the region, no short-term cuts. There are no quarterly results to defend, but the values are strict.

## What was sold in pre-sales

The contract was signed by the pharmaceutical operations director. It covers **12 weeks at the dermo-cosmetics plant in Gascony**, with a team of four FDEs (you).

**Scope in the signed proposal:** "Cut the time from end of packaging to batch release from 18 days to 10. An AI assistant reviews batch records, flags anomalies and prepares deviation investigations. In the target state, conforming batches are released automatically."

The group quality director, who is also the plant's **qualified person** (the pharmacist who legally certifies each batch before it leaves), did not attend the pre-sales meetings.
